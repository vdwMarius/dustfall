# Phase 3 packaged playtest

Launch `G:\ue\dustfall\Builds\Phase3\Windows\Play_DUSTFALL.cmd` after packaging.
Keep the full Windows folder. The launcher uses 1920x1080 High; Alt+F4 exits.
Record build commit, hardware, settings and PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED for each item.

- Start inside the furnished habitat without clipping or falling. Check both
  camera views, movement, jump, F flashlight and sustained Shift sprint.
- Press E near the inner airlock. Walk into the chamber while that door is open,
  wait for the seal, then leave through the outer door. Return with E outside.
  The opposite door should stay closed. Standing in a doorway should pause
  closure without trapping or crushing the player.
- Oxygen and power decrease outside and refill inside. Stamina decreases while
  sprinting and limits speed when exhausted, then recovers. Storm wear affects
  integrity outside. Confirm the suit HUD stays legible and reliable.
- Check daytime ground detail and dense, warm airborne dust. At measured
  distances around 40, 60 and 100 m, report whether recognizable silhouettes
  persist while detail fades. Check calm/haze/storm and night with the flashlight.
  Report opaque dust planes, extreme glare, excessive darkness or fog indoors.
- Walk around small stones, composite boulders and cliff formations from several
  sides. Look for floating bases, visible open scan backs, stretched textures,
  obvious copy patterns and collision traps. Small scatter is nonblocking.
- Visit the exterior wreck, ruin and cruiser routes, then return through the
  airlock. These ship/ruin landmarks are still exterior prototypes; full ship
  exploration and final suit art are not completed in this module.
- F5 save, move away, F9 load: location and suit state should restore. Exit and
  relaunch to check automatic restore and consistent environment placement.
- For the death loop, remain outside until oxygen fails (about 14 minutes from
  full oxygen at default rates). Confirm a new clone number and safe home spawn,
  with one marked recovery cache at the death site. Approach and press E to
  recover once. The payload is currently a minimal scrap-count contract; normal
  gameplay starts with no carried items until Phase 4 scavenging is implemented.
- Listen for wind continuity and low-oxygen warning volume/cooldown. Telemetry
  checks do not establish subjective audio acceptance.
- Note stutters during first exposure to materials/effects, rapid turns, storms
  and landmark approaches. Record frame times/FPS and the location/weather.

Automated checks exercise runtime rules and injected controls. Physical keys,
subjective visibility/audio and representative hardware acceptance need this
packaged playtest. Do not mark untested items as passed.
